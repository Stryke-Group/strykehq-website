01 / The approach

Find the friction.
Build only what helps.

Most teams don’t need more software. They need a clearer path through the work they already do.

01 — Understand

Choose one recurring problem.

Walk through the request, the people involved, the tools you use and where the next step gets lost.

02 — Shape

Test the smallest useful change.

Start with a workflow map, a checklist or a focused prototype. Use public or anonymized examples first.

03 — Hand over

Leave a process people can use.

Agree on the review checks, ownership and instructions before adding more automation.

Before we start

Small scope.
Clear expectations.

Do we need to replace our existing tools?

That is not the starting point. We first look at the tools and process you already use. Any new tool or integration needs an agreed reason and scope.

Is there a finished Stryke software product?

The longer-term product direction is exploratory. A first engagement is a separately scoped workflow service, not access to a launched Super App.

What does a pilot cost?

Scope, deliverables, timing and pricing are agreed after a fit review. There is no automatic subscription or commitment from an inquiry.

What should I share in my first message?

One recurring problem, who handles it and what a useful outcome would look like. Keep customer records, credentials and private files out of the first message.

Stryke / Bangladesh

Rooted in Dhaka.
Focused on useful work.

Stryke began with a Dhaka marketplace idea in 2020. Today, this Bangladesh-first, founder-led practice is exploring how AI can support the work around customer inquiries without removing human judgment.

The focus

Inquiry to next action.

Bring context together, prepare a response for review and make the next responsibility clear. Final sending and approvals stay with your team.

The evidence

Examples before promises.

Synthetic examples have been tested to explore the approach. They are demonstrations, not customer results or evidence of a live client deployment.

The fit

One problem, well defined.

Discuss the recurring task, the people responsible and your existing tools. Any proposed pilot needs separately agreed scope, timing and deliverables.
