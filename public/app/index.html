Useful context.

The longer-term idea: bring AI conversations, bounded agent work and human review into a clearer operating surface. This is a product direction, not a launched software service.

The principles

Keep the work connected.
Keep the decisions accountable.

Context

Start with what matters.

The goal, sources, privacy limits and expected output travel with the request.

Assistance

Give AI a bounded role.

Use chatbots and agents to prepare and organize. Do not confuse a suggestion with an approved action.

Review

Make the human checkpoint visible.

A person checks the output before it becomes a message, commitment or external action.

Service first

Start with the workflow.
Let useful patterns earn the product.

No product-access, integration or release-date promise is made here. A workflow discussion can help establish what is worth testing.
