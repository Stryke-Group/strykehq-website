Stryke Social Publishing

Privacy Policy

Our limited internal TikTok identity connection.

Who we are

Stryke (“we,” “us,” or “our”) uses Stryke Social Publishing for an authorised internal TikTok identity connection, not a public publishing service. This policy applies only to Stryke Social Publishing, including visits to its notices on our website; it does not replace notices for other Stryke services. You can use our contact page or contact us about your information at hello@stryke.com.bd.

Information and purpose

When you authorise the connection, we use Composio to obtain your TikTok app-scoped identifier, display name and avatar information. We use these to identify the connected account and verify that the basic identity connection works. Avatar information can include a link to your profile image.

The authorised purpose does not include posting, listing videos, reading statistics or direct messages, marketing, or unrelated profiling. We do not ask you to submit sensitive details or private content for this connection.

Connection records and operational logs may also contain account or connection references, the action performed, timestamps, status and error information. Composio stores request and response payloads in execution logs by default, so returned identity information may appear in those logs. We do not claim that zero-data-retention settings apply to this connection.

Website essentials and requests

Our website uses browser storage to remember your theme preference. Our hosting and security infrastructure may process technical information such as your IP address, browser and request details, timestamps and security events to deliver pages, troubleshoot problems and protect the site. Contacting us also provides the contact details and message you choose to send; we use them to respond and handle your request. These website and contact activities are separate from granting TikTok permissions.

How the connection and providers work

TikTok supplies the identity information and authorisation mechanism. Composio handles the connected account’s OAuth tokens and uses them to authenticate requests to TikTok. These tokens are different from the app credentials used to identify our application. You should not send us your TikTok password, app secrets or tokens by email.

We work with TikTok, Composio and the service providers we use to operate and review this connection. Our operational tools, including automated assistants, may process basic identity results and connection records for that purpose. Only authorised Stryke personnel and service providers may handle this information to operate the connection or resolve connection and privacy requests. Provider infrastructure may process information outside your country; processing is not limited to Stryke or to one location.

For their own processing and retention practices, see Composio’s Privacy Policy, Composio’s data-retention documentation, and the privacy policy applicable to your account available through TikTok. These provider policies are separate from this notice.

Retention and deletion

Retention can differ between identity information, connection records, OAuth tokens, operational logs and backups. We do not specify a single fixed retention period for all of these records. Information may remain while needed to operate or troubleshoot the authorised connection, address requests, or meet applicable legal requirements. Provider-held records follow the relevant provider’s retention rules and available deletion processes.

You may request access, correction or deletion of information relating to this connection by emailing hello@stryke.com.bd. Include only the information needed to identify your request, not passwords, tokens or sensitive documents. We may need to confirm that the request relates to your account. We review requests and explain any legal, technical or provider-related limits. Some retained logs or backups may not be erased immediately. This does not limit any rights you have under applicable law.

Your choice and revoking access

You can decline to authorise the connection or ask us to disconnect it through the business contact above. You can also use the app-permission controls available in TikTok to withdraw access.

Disconnecting the integration or revoking authorisation is different from deleting information already received. Revocation does not by itself erase provider records, logs or backups. Please contact us separately if you also want deletion considered.

Safeguards and changes

No online service can eliminate every security risk. Please report concerns through our business contact without including passwords, tokens or sensitive content.

We may update this policy as the limited connection changes. Any broader use would need to be described before it begins; authorising this basic identity connection does not authorise broader TikTok access.
