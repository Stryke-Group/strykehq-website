A clear path from
request to next action.

A workflow does not need to be complicated to be useful. It needs enough context, a sensible review point and someone who owns what happens next.

This is an example of a process to discuss, not a live workspace or a connected CRM.

  1. 01

    Capture

    Record the request, source, owner and desired outcome.

  2. 02

    Context

    Gather relevant references, constraints and public-safe examples.

  3. 03

    Prepare

    Use existing tools and bounded AI assistance to shape a useful response.

  4. 04

    Review

    Check accuracy, privacy, tone and permission before taking action.

  5. 05

    Hand over

    Give the next action a named owner and an agreed date. Keep the instructions reusable.
